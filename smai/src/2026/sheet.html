Quick checks on an answer

  • A probability lies between 0 and 1, and the posteriors of all the classes add up to 1.
  • A covariance matrix is symmetric, and its diagonal (the variances) is never negative.
  • Eigenvalues of a symmetric PSD matrix (\(X\T X\), a covariance) are never negative. They add up to the trace.
  • Singular values are never negative, and there are at most \(\min(m, n)\) non-zero ones.
  • Precision, recall and accuracy lie between 0 and 1. Recall cannot change if you only change how many negatives there are.
  • After mean-centring, the new points add up to the zero vector.
  • A gradient descent step on a convex bowl should lower the loss. If it rises, the learning rate is too big or the sign is wrong.
  • Substitute a closed-form \(w\) back into the data. For points that lie exactly on a line, the fit should go through every point.
  • Check a convolution output size by counting positions on a small grid, and check a parameter count against PyTorch.
