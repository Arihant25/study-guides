# SD&ET Study Guide (Detection Theory)

Study guide for EC5.406 Signal Detection and Estimation Theory (the detection half), by Varun Shastry and Arihant Tripathy.

**Live site:** https://arihant25.github.io/study-guides/sdet/

Fifteen chapters in lecture order, from the likelihood ratio test to Wald's sequential test. Every proof done in class is written out, and every homework and classwork problem is solved. Chapter 13 solves Assignment 4, the Nov 2026 end-sem paper (section 13.5), and the detection questions from the 2024 and 2025 papers. Chapter 14 is the formula sheet.

Static HTML: `index.html` with `guide.css`, `figures.js` and `assets/`. KaTeX loads from a CDN.
