"Find the errors in this diagram" questions

  1. Check the axes first: is energy on the vertical axis and the right quantity on the horizontal? Are the labels on the right curves?
  2. Check each named feature against the rule you know: spacing \(\propto n^2\), wavefunctions zero at walls, \(n - 1\) nodes, a bulk gap in a TI, the Dirac (not "Pauli") point, R rising for metals and falling for semiconductors, a sudden drop to zero at \(T_c\), an insulator in an MTJ.
  3. Number each error, say why it is wrong in one sentence, then draw the corrected diagram. The question usually says how many errors there are; find that many.

Quick checks on your answers

  • A probability lies between 0 and 1. \(|\psi|^2\) is never negative. A normalised box state has \(A = \sqrt{2/L}\).
  • A speed above \(3\times10^8\) m/s is impossible; if you get one, the situation is impossible (as for the electron in a nucleus) or you made an error.
  • Photon energies of visible light are 1.6 to 3.1 eV (780 to 400 nm). Band gaps of semiconductors are about 0.5 to 3 eV.
  • \(k_BT\) at room temperature is 0.026 eV. A charging energy or gap much bigger than this is stable at room temperature.
  • In GMR and TMR, \(R_{AP} > R_P\), so the ratio is positive.
  • Density of states powers: 3D \(+\frac12\), 2D \(0\), 1D \(-\frac12\). Check the units: per energy per volume, area or length.
  • Write the formula, then the numbers, then the answer with units, in that order. Each line earns marks.
