One day left?

Follow this order and you will have seen everything once. The times add up to about seven hours, so take breaks between blocks.

  1. 45 minutes. Read the "In plain words" box at the top of each of the four parts. Don't read the proofs yet. The aim is to know what each topic is about.
  2. 3 hours on BA (items 1 to 3 above). This is half the syllabus. Learn the rate and \(P(k)\) first, then the master equation, then the three variants. Do the BA practice questions after each one.
  3. 90 minutes on the small world (items 4 and 5), then its practice questions.
  4. 1 hour on sync and Lorenz (items 6 and 7). They are short, and the numbers questions are easy marks.
  5. 1 hour at the end. Do the past questions with the page closed, then run the flashcards.

In the exam, write every step even if you remember the answer. The 2026 mid-sem gave 0 for a correct formula with no derivation.

How to use this page

There are 12 proofs. Half of them (the BA family) share one method, so it is less than it looks.

  1. Learn the formulas in the next section until you can write each one with its "where" line.
  2. Start with BA (Part 3). Learn the rate \(\frac{dk_i}{dt}\), then \(P(k)\) by continuum theory. Models A and B and the nonlinear case reuse that method with a different \(\Pi\). You write \(\frac{dk_i}{dt} = m\Pi\) and integrate from \(k_i(t_i) = m\), and where \(P(k)\) is needed, the uniform joining times give it.
  3. Then the master equation. It is the longest proof, and the board names it, so write it out at least twice.
  4. Next, the small world (Part 4). Learn \(C\) and \(\bar\ell\) at \(p = 0\) and \(p = 1\), then \((1-p)^3\).
  5. Synchronisation and Lorenz (Parts 1 and 2) need one write-through each, since you learned them for Quiz 2.
  6. Finally, do the past questions with the page closed, then the flashcards.

For every proof, learn the shaded one-line idea first, then the steps. The 2026 mid-sem gave 0 for a formula with no derivation, so always write the steps, even when you remember the answer.
